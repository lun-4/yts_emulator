# DMNware Apps

Each app under `Gen/<AppName>/` is three files (`<App>.cs`, `<App>.layout.cs`, `<App>.gen.cs`) plus a generated prefab. To author or modify an app, **read `../AppGen/CLAUDE.md` first** — it covers the cold-start cycle, the layout DSL, the UdonSharp whitelist gotchas, and the final user-side install steps.
